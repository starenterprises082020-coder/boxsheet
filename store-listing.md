## Full description (4000 characters max)

BoxSheet turns four dimensions into a proper key line diagram and an honest cost per box.

**Key line diagrams in seconds**
Enter length, width, height and the board, and get a dimensioned flat drawing with cut and crease lines, a closed-box view, and an engineering title block with drawing number, revision, tolerance and signature fields. Download it as a PDF and send it to your vendor or file it for audit. No rates needed.

**Know what a box should cost**
Paper layer by layer with take-up on the fluting, deckle rounded to the reel sizes actually sold, trim costed honestly, conversion, printing, finishing, overhead and freight. For printed inner boxes it works out how many blanks fit a 28 x 40 press sheet, allowing for the gripper, and charges the impressions accordingly.

**Check a vendor quote**
Enter their rate. See the gap as a percentage and in rupees across the whole order.

**Strength before you order**
Bursting strength from GSM and BF, compression strength from the McKee formula, and a warning when the board will not carry your own stack or meet the strength written on the drawing.

**Made for the plant**
- Kraft outer boxes and printed duplex inner boxes
- Boxes that open on the top or on the end
- Inner or outer sizes, millimetres or inches
- One piece or two piece sheets
- Saved boxes with automatic revision history
- One rate master: change a paper rate once and every box re-costs
- Backup and restore to move to a new phone

**Your rates stay yours**
Everything is stored on your device. No account, no login, no analytics, nothing uploaded. The app works offline.

Built by a working plant head, not by a software company.
