Privacy policy

BoxSheet · last updated 25 September 2026

BoxSheet is a calculator and drawing tool for corrugated and duplex boxes. It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

What is stored, and where

Everything you enter — box sizes, board specifications, paper and conversion rates, saved boxes, drawing numbers and company details — is stored only on the device you enter it on, in the browser or app's own local storage. It is not uploaded anywhere. There is no account and no login.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no analytics, no advertising, no trackers and no crash reporting. We cannot see your rates, your drawings or how you use the app.

Files you create

PDF drawings, costings and backup files are created on your device and saved wherever you choose. They are never sent to us.

Network

The app works offline after the first load. It may fetch fonts from Google Fonts on first use; after that it runs from its own cache.

Deleting your data

Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling it, removes everything permanently. Take a backup first if you want to keep it.

Children

The app is a workplace tool and is not directed at children.
